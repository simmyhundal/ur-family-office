Is the Joint alert bot safe?

A small program on Simmy's Mac checks the Joint account every Monday and posts one line in our private Telegram group, so nobody has to ask "do we have enough for the card?"

It can

  • Post a status line in our group
  • Read balances and card statements, read-only

It cannot

  • Move money or touch the bank
  • Read our chat or answer anyone
  • Post anywhere except our group

What the messages say

Telegram groups are not end-to-end encrypted, so messages stay thin. Minimal gives a status and a date. Headroom adds our cushion and the lowest balance, rounded down to $500. Exact balances never go in the chat.

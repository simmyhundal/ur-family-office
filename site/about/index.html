About

A small, careful tool for comparing your investments with the market.

How it works

  • Nothing is uploaded. The math runs in your browser. The mix you enter, and any CSV you load, never leave your device. There are no accounts, no analytics and no trackers.
  • Prices are month-end adjusted closes, which count dividends as reinvested. They are fetched on weekdays and bundled with the site.
  • The cash rate used for the Sharpe ratio is the 3-month US Treasury bill rate from FRED, the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis data service.

Limits

  • Monthly data only, so daily swings are not captured.
  • Before taxes, fees and trading costs. Deposits are assumed at month-end.
  • A short history can show almost anything, and past results don’t predict future ones.
  • US funds and US dollars only. The sample portfolio is an invented person.

Information, not investment advice

UFO is for personal and educational use. It does not recommend that you buy or sell anything, and it is not a licensed adviser. Talk to a qualified professional before making financial decisions.

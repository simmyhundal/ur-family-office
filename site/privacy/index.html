Privacy

Short version: what you type into the tool stays in your browser.

Draft, pending legal review. This page describes what the site does today. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and may change before the site is opened to the public.

What stays in your browser

The mix of funds, amounts and any prices file you load are worked out on your device. They are not uploaded, and there is no account or server that could receive them. The tool keeps your latest mix in this tab only (session storage) so a lesson can use it; closing the tab clears it.

What the sign-up form sends

Only if you submit the sign-up form: your email address, your role (student, instructor or other), your school if you give one, the plan you are interested in, and that you consented. It goes to the form service the site is configured with, and nowhere else. No portfolio data is ever attached. We use it to email you about early access.

You can ask us to delete your entry at any time by emailing the contact address in the footer once it is published, or by opening an issue on the project page.

No analytics, no cookies

The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or trackers. It loads no third-party scripts, fonts or images. Price and rate data are files served from the same site. GitHub Pages, which hosts the site, keeps its own server logs under its own policy.

Payments

Nothing is sold yet. When payments open, they will happen on the payment provider’s own page; card details never touch this site.

Information, not investment advice.

Information, not investment advice. Results are before taxes and trading costs, and past results don’t predict future ones.

Prices: month-end adjusted closes, refreshed on weekdays. Cash rate: 3-month Treasury bills from FRED.
